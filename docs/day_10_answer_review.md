## Review rules

- Do not change relevance labels because the critic or reviser prefers a different chunk.
- A critic `accept` verdict is not proof that an answer is correct.
- Record unnecessary revisions as failures, not improvements.
- Record any final `revise` verdict after the one allowed revision as unresolved.
- Compare answer quality as well as citation metrics and token/model-call cost.
